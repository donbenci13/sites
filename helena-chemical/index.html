Product Portfolio

Everything the Modern Grower Needs

Helena's product lineup spans every category of crop inputs, backed by rigorous field testing and agronomic support.

🛡️ Crop Protection

Herbicides, fungicides, insecticides, and adjuvants — including proprietary Helena formulations developed in-house for specific regional challenges.

🌾 Seed & Traits

Corn, soybeans, cotton, wheat, rice, sorghum, and specialty crop seed with advanced genetics. Backed by Helena's own seed research program.

🧪 Plant Nutrition

Complete fertilizer lines including dry, liquid, and suspension formulations. Helena's custom blending program matches any soil test recommendation.

🚁 Application Services

Aerial and ground application using GPS-guided equipment. Variable rate technology, site-specific spraying, and 24-hour service windows during season.

📊 Precision Platform

Helena's proprietary ag data platform gives growers real-time field insights, application maps, and yield analytics in one dashboard.

🌍 Sustainability

Carbon credit advisory, 4R nutrient stewardship, cover crop support, and reduced-carbon input options for growers pursuing sustainability goals.
