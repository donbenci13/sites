Most Common Types of Crop Insurance

Revenue Protection (RP)

An RP policy protects a policyholder's income when the crop insurance revenue falls below the guaranteed crop insurance revenue. It provides coverage to protect against loss of revenue caused by low prices, low yields or a combination of both.

Area Risk Protection (ARP)

An ARP policy is a county-based insurance product that pays the policyholder in the event the actual county revenue falls below the county trigger revenue selected by the policyholder.

Hail (Private Products)

Provides a loss payment for any loss to the insured crop due to an insured peril like wind, hail, yield loss, plus many more. Contact us to learn more about this, or any other, coverage option.
