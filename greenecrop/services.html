What We Offer

From soil analysis to crop insurance, Greene Crop Consulting provides end-to-end services that help Indiana farmers maximize their productivity and profitability. Each service is customized to your operation's specific goals and challenges.

🌾

Crop Consulting

Maintaining land value starts with proper agronomic planning. We help allocate fertilizer dollars efficiently through comprehensive soil sampling, fertility recommendations, nitrogen sampling, and regular scouting. Our services also include VR prescription writing and yield map processing to maximize your return on every acre.

Agronomy Solutions

Identify crop disease, nutrient deficiencies, and pest pressures early with our expert agronomy services. We offer detailed soil analysis, drone services for aerial field assessments, and comprehensive scouting programs to keep your crops healthy and productive throughout the season.

Crop Insurance

Protect your operation with Revenue Protection, Area Risk Protection, and Hail insurance products. Through our partnerships with Diversified, Rain and Hail, and NAU, we help you find the right coverage to safeguard your investment and provide peace of mind.
