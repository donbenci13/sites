Why Cover Crops

Building Better Soil, Season by Season

Cover crops protect against erosion, suppress weeds, improve water infiltration, and build organic matter. We help you select the right seed and strategy for your fields.

Soil HealthReduce erosion, build organic matter, and improve soil structure with targeted cover crop species.
Nutrient ManagementCatch and recycle nutrients, fix nitrogen, and reduce fertilizer needs with smart crop rotations.

Seed Offerings

Premium Cover Crop Seed

CR

Cereal Rye

Fast-growing winter hardy cereal that provides excellent biomass, weed suppression, and erosion control.

CC

Crimson Clover

Winter annual legume that fixes nitrogen and produces vibrant red blooms. Excellent for pollinator support and green manure.

HV

Hairy Vetch

Cold-tolerant legume that fixes significant nitrogen. Ideal partner for cereal rye in spring termination mixes.

OT

Oats

Fast-establishing spring or fall cereal that produces quick ground cover and readily terminates over winter.

RD

Radish

Deep-tapping brassica that breaks compaction, scavenges nutrients, and provides quick fall biomass.
