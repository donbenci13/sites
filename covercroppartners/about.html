About

Your Partner in Soil Health

Our Mission

Cover Crop Partners provides premium seed, expert guidance, and practical solutions that help growers improve soil health, boost yields, and farm more sustainably.

Our Approach

We believe every farm is different. That is why we take the time to understand your rotation, soil type, and goals before recommending a seed strategy — no cookie-cutter solutions.

Our Commitment

Quality matters. We source high-germination, regionally adapted seed and stand behind every pound. Your success in the field is our success.

Our Story

Built on Experience and Trust

Cover Crop Partners LLC was founded to fill a need in the agricultural community — reliable access to high-quality cover crop seed and honest, knowledgeable advice. Based in Shipshewana, Indiana, we serve growers across the region who are committed to building better soil for the long haul.
