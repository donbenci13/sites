How It Works

From Planning to Planting

1. Consultation

We discuss your goals, fields, rotation, and timeline to recommend the right species and seeding approach for your operation.

2. Seed Selection

Choose from premium single species or let us build a custom mix. We source only high-quality, regionally adapted seed.

3. Establishment

We coordinate seeding via no-till drill or aerial application, ensuring even distribution and good soil contact for strong emergence.
