About Us

The KOVA Agribusiness Team

For over seven decades, Kova Fertilizer Inc. has been a cornerstone of Midwest agriculture. Founded in Greensburg, Indiana, we've grown from a single fertilizer supplier into a diversified agribusiness team serving farmers across multiple states.

The KOVA Agribusiness Team (KAT) provides a unique network of support — from crop fertility specialists and agribusiness finance to wholesale supply and retail solutions. We believe one solution doesn't fit every operation, so we offer a collaborative team approach rooted in proven values.

  • Family-owned legacy
  • Agronomic expertise
  • Reliable supply chain
  • Community partnership
