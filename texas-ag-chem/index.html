About Us

Rooted in the South Plains

Founded in 1998, Texas Ag Chemicals has been a trusted supplier of crop inputs to the agricultural community of the South Plains region for over two decades.

We understand the unique growing conditions of West Texas — from the sandy loam soils of the High Plains to the caliche clay of the Rolling Plains. Our team works closely with growers to develop customized fertility and crop protection programs that maximize yield and profitability.

  • Custom fertilizer blending
  • Crop protection expertise
  • Soil testing & analysis
  • Local family owned
