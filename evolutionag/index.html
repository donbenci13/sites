Our Services

Agronomy Solutions for Every Season

From planting through harvest, Evolution Ag provides the products and expertise to protect your crop and maximize your return on every acre.

CP

Crop Protection

Full line of herbicides, fungicides, and insecticides from leading manufacturers. We help you select the right product for your weed spectrum, disease pressure, and crop stage.

F

Fertilizer & Nutrition

Custom fertility programs using dry and liquid fertilizer, micronutrients, and lime. Tailored to your soil test results and yield goals for optimal plant nutrition.

S

Seed

High-performance corn and soybean seed varieties matched to your field conditions, maturity zone, and management practices for consistent, top-end yields.

CA

Custom Application

Precision application services for crop protection products and fertilizer using modern equipment calibrated for accuracy and efficiency across your fields.

SC

Field Scouting

Regular field monitoring throughout the growing season to identify pest, disease, and nutrient issues early — before they impact your bottom line.

AG

Agronomic Consulting

One-on-one consultation to build a season-long plan covering variety selection, input timing, fertility strategy, and harvest management.
