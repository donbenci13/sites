What We Offer

Comprehensive Research Services

From field trial design to advanced statistical modeling, we provide end-to-end research support tailored to the unique challenges of modern agriculture.

Consultation

Strategic research consultation covering trial design, protocol development, regulatory compliance, and crop management planning. We help you ask the right questions and design studies that produce meaningful answers.

Protocol DesignRegulatoryCrop Planning

Statistical Analysis

Rigorous statistical modeling and data interpretation using SAS, R, and ARM. Services include ANOVA, mixed models, spatial analysis, meta-analysis, and customized reporting for publication or regulatory submission.

ANOVAMixed ModelsSAS / RARM

Plant Breeding

Expert plant breeding services including germplasm evaluation, line development, hybrid testing, marker-assisted selection, and variety trial management across multiple environments and geographies.

GermplasmHybrid TestingMarker SelectionVariety Trials
