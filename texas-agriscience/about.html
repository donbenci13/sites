About

Family-Owned Research Farm in Deep South Texas

Texas AgriScience, LLC started in 2018 and focuses on practical agriculture research and contract breeding support.

Jacob Pekar, Ph.D.

Researcher, breeder, consultant, and owner. Jacob earned a Ph.D. in plant breeding from Texas A&M University in 2020, with a maize aflatoxin resistance focus. He also holds a master's in Weed Science from Texas A&M Kingsville and a B.S. in Agronomy from Texas Tech University.

Audrey Pekar

Financial coordinator and co-owner. Audrey oversees company financials and supports daily farm operations across multiple functions.

Martin Barroso

Research technician with over 30 years of experience, especially in cotton. Martin has supported many Texas research programs and joined Texas AgriScience in 2019.
