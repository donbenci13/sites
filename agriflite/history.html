History

Soaring Across Generations

Abridged company milestones from the Agriflite historical record.

  • 1973

    First swath flown with a Cessna 188. Early work focused on insecticide and fungicide application.

  • 1976

    Fleet expanded with an Air Tractor 301.

  • 1995

    AeroFlow Systems, a leak-free spray system, was developed.

  • 2001–2004

    Next generation leadership joined the business as ag pilots.

  • 2006

    AgSync order management software launched; fungicide demand increased sharply.

  • 2012

    VeriFly GPS guidance was developed and hangar operations were upgraded.

  • 2013

    Partnership established with AgTech, applicators for Nutrien Ag Solutions.

  • 2015

    King Aero Aviation acquired in Elkhart, Rochester, and Wakarusa. Delta Airlines “Puffer” award received from NAAA.

  • 2023

    Agriflite celebrated 50 years of service.

  • 2024

    King Aero sold, Venture Aviation Services founded, and AeroFlow transitioned to longtime employees.

  • 2025

    A new chapter begins while honoring founder legacy and continuing the company mission.
