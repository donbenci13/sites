About Us

Generations of Proven Service

Agriflite Services Inc has been proudly serving the agricultural community of Wakarusa and beyond since 1973. What started as a small family operation has grown into a trusted regional resource.

With more than 50 years in the business, we understand the land, the seasons, and what it takes to keep a farm running smoothly. Our team brings hands-on experience and a deep commitment to quality service.

We treat every customer like family — because in a close-knit community like Wakarusa, that's exactly what you are. Whether you need custom application, crop consulting, or agronomic support, we have the expertise and equipment to get the job done right.

What We Do

Professional Ag Services

We offer a full range of agricultural services to help your farm thrive — from custom application to precision agronomy.

Custom Application

Precision fertilizer, lime, and chemical application using modern equipment for uniform coverage and maximum efficiency.

Agronomy & Crop Consulting

Expert recommendations on seed selection, nutrient management, pest control, and crop rotation tailored to your fields.

Soil Testing & Analysis

Comprehensive soil sampling and lab analysis to determine exact nutrient needs and optimize your input investment.

Seed & Input Supply

Quality seed, fertilizer, and crop protection products from leading manufacturers, with knowledgeable advice on every purchase.

Equipment Service

Reliable application and spreading equipment available, with regular maintenance to ensure precision and dependability in the field.

Grain Marketing Support

Guidance on grain marketing strategies, storage recommendations, and market analysis to help maximize your operation's profitability.
